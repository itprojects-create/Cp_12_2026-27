# How quant algos make money — and why Quant-Plus isn't profitable (yet): an honest gap analysis

*Synthesized 2026-11-07 from four research-note files in research_notes/How quant algos make money/ — see Sources.*

## Executive summary: the money is in flow you don't have, and our test hasn't been taken yet

The dominant quant profit mechanisms take money from counterparties an individual cannot be: un/ill-informed flow (retail order internalization paid brokers ~$0.20 per 100 shares and gave market makers "100% more revenue" per option trade than per equity trade ([Wharton WFFI](https://wffi.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Payment-for-Order-Flow-final.pdf))), quote-suppliers losing races measured in microseconds won overwhelmingly by six firms ([BIS WP 955](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-955-quantifying-high-frequency-trading-arms-race)), and in India specifically a retail F&O pool that lost ₹91,685 crore in FY26 while **96–97% of prop/FPI profits were algo-generated** ([ET on SEBI's Aug-2026 study](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/stocks/news/rs-91685-crore-gone-88-retail-investors-lost-money-in-fo-trading-in-fy26-even-after-strict-sebi-rules/articleshow/133381520.cms), [CernoQuant](https://cernoquant.com/statistics)). Quant-Plus sits in the one mechanism class that is retail-accessible — daily directional forecasting, long-only, full delivery costs — and that is the lowest-margin class in the catalog. The ranked gaps: **(1) no evidence base yet** — 320 locked forecast cards but only 2 settled outcomes, so no claim about the model is currently testable; **(2) realistic edge size vs Indian costs** — a competent IC≈0.05 signal earns ~0.2% per trade against a ~0.4% round-trip hurdle; **(3) objective mismatch** — forecast accuracy and net P&L are different objectives, and the published proof that "more accurate, less money" is real costs us nothing to respect; **(4) the gated mechanics** — market making, latency arb, PFOF and premium capture need colocation, licenses and capital we don't have; **(5) the availability leak** — 5 of 6 scheduled evening runs since Oct 30 were lost, and forward-only design makes every lost run permanently lost evidence; **(6) the bridge's HALT is correct conduct, not failure** — the profitability test simply hasn't been taken yet. The honest path is mechanical, not motivational: keep the machine on, let the cards settle, unlock calibration at ~50 settles, let the pre-registered paper stream accumulate ~30+ net-of-cost picks, and only then — if the mean realized excess clears 0.4% — consider tiny real capital. If it doesn't clear after 1–2 full settled horizons, the honest conclusion is that this strategy class does not clear Indian delivery costs, and the evidence points to specific alternatives.

## Where the money physically comes from: each mechanism has a named loser

The most useful way to read the quant business is not by strategy names but by who pays. **High-frequency market-making** (~80% of HFT firms by strategy) quotes both sides and earns the spread at massive turnover ([Systemic Risk DP 113](https://www.systemicrisk.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2022-01/dp-113.pdf)); the empirical literature shows it lowers spreads while shifting cost onto end-users executing large institutional orders ([EFMA 2020](https://www.efmaefm.org/0EFMAMEETINGS/EFMA%20ANNUAL%20MEETINGS/2020-Dublin/papers/EFMA%202020_stage-1301_question-Full%20Paper_id-422.pdf), [Menkveld](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/86769/1/11-076.pdf)). **Latency arbitrage** — races of 5–10 microseconds, ~20–22% of volume, top 6 firms winning >80% of races — extracts a "tax" worth £51–63m/year in UK equities (2014–2017 data), extrapolating to roughly $5bn/year globally ([BIS WP 955](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-955-quantifying-high-frequency-trading-arms-race), [NBER w29011](https://ideas.repec.org/p/nbr/nberwo/29011.html)). **Retail order internalization** concentrates flow so heavily that one wholesaler plus two peers account for most US PFOF ([NBER w29883](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w29883/w29883.pdf)); options are the rich leg of that business ([Wharton WFFI](https://wffi.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Payment-for-Order-Flow-final.pdf)). **Stat-arb** harvests liquidity-demanding flow — notably predictable options-market-maker hedging ([Systemic Risk DP 113](https://www.systemicrisk.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2022-01/dp-113.pdf)). **Volatility desks** earn premium from impatient option buyers. India's coarsest single datapoint on who pays: FY24 gross P&L of prop desks +₹33,000 crore and FPIs +₹28,000 crore versus individuals −₹61,000+ crore, with 96–97% of the winners' profits algo-generated (SEBI participant data as tabulated at [CernoQuant](https://cernoquant.com/statistics)).

The reward figures stratify sharply. Renaissance Medallion's ~39–40% net CAGR since 1988 (66% gross) is author-insider-reported, **never audited**, and structurally immovable — employee-only since 1993, AUM capped ~$10–15B ([Wikipedia RenTec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance_Technologies), [Quartr](https://quartr.com/insights/edge/renaissance-technologies-and-the-medallion-fund), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/greatest-fund-ever-why-jim-142555191.html)). The investable quant tier delivers claimed (press-sourced, not audited) mid-teens: AQR Apex +19.6%, D.E. Shaw Composite +18.5%, Citadel Wellington +10.2% in 2025 ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/01/02/top-hedge-fund-performers-2025-bridgewater-de-shaw-citadel-millennium)). The genuinely verifiable numbers are market-maker revenues from statutory/lender filings: **Jane Street $39.6B net trading revenue in 2025** — "the highest single-year trading result on record for any firm" ([Reuters, Apr 2026](https://www.reuters.com/world/jane-streets-40-billion-trading-haul-tops-rivals-sources-say-2026-04-24)), XTX £3.93B with ~250 staff ([Finance Magnates, 2026](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/xtx-markets-revenue-rises-43-to-393-billion-in-2025)), Optiver €4.556B ([Optiver annual report](https://www.optiver.com/insights/news/optiver-reports-robust-financial-results-for-2025)), Citadel Securities $12.2B ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/jane-streets-40-billion-trading-haul-tops-rivals-sources-say-2026-04-24)). India's own book: SEBI's July 2025 interim order estimates Jane Street earned **~₹32,681 crore in the examination window**, with ₹4,843.5 crore frozen in the alleged expiry-day manipulation matter ([SEBI order, Jul 3 2025](https://www.sebi.gov.in/enforcement/orders/jul-2025/interim-order-in-the-matter-of-index-manipulation-by-jane-street-group_95040.html), [FTI analysis](https://www.fticonsulting.com/insights/articles/when-algorithmic-trading-meets-allegations-market-manipulation)); domestic firms' filings show Graviton profit +17% to ~₹1,200 crore, HRT India +156% to ~₹22 billion, AlphaGrep +77% to ₹474 crore (fiscal labels partly ambiguous) ([ET/Bloomberg, Oct 2026](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/stocks/news/high-frequency-firms-see-india-profits-surge-despite-regulatory-curbs/articleshow/125554761.cms)).

## The moat is entitlements and message data, not code

Speed-class profits are gated by what you physically occupy and are entitled to see. Latency-arb races live in exchange *message* data (failed orders visible only there) and are winnable only from inside a colocation fabric ([BIS WP 955](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-955-quantifying-high-frequency-trading-arms-race)); US sub-500-microsecond setups cost a vendor-estimated $200,000–400,000 upfront plus $36,000–72,000/month ([WesternPips, vendor estimate](https://westernpips.com/hft-infrastructure-cost.html)) — though India's floor is far lower at NSE's half-rack ₹4.5 lakh one-time + ₹50,000/month with an 18.9µs rack-to-router reference ([NSE colo page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/trade/platform-services-co-location-facility), [Endovia](https://www.endoviawealth.com/how-latency-affects-trade-execution-lessons-from-the-pros)). The compute/data class is gated by XTX-style stacks — ~$250B/day notional across 50,000+ instruments, **25,000 GPUs** in the research cluster ([Jawlah](https://jawlah.co/en/63361)). Even India's "retail algo" regime is broker-railed: static IP, exchange-assigned algo ID, ≤10 orders/sec threshold ([SEBI circular, Feb 2025](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html), [NSE INVG/67858](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INVG67858.pdf)). What an individual can replicate: long-horizon factor tilts, cross-asset trend/momentum logic that replicates publicly ([Man Group](https://www.man.com/insights/trend-following-equity-and-bond-crisis-alpha)), mid-frequency stat-arb on liquid names, ETF/iNAV bases ([Paytm Money](https://www.paytmmoney.com/blog/etf-pricing-nav-inav-fair-value-explained)). What is closed: microsecond market-making, internalization, full-depth Indian F&O books, and 25,000-GPU research. A daily-directional LLM forecast book is in the replicable class — which is exactly why it pays the least.

## India charges ~0.24% before slippage, and the documented survivors are positional

The delivery cost ledger (Zerodha's live schedule, checked 7 Nov 2026) is unambiguous: STT 0.1% per side dominates, and a ₹1 lakh delivery round trip costs **≈₹237.6 ≈ 0.238%** before slippage ([Zerodha charges](https://zerodha.com/charges/)); intraday is ~0.106% ([Zerodha charges](https://zerodha.com/charges/)). Documented large-cap slippage runs ~5–20 bps per trade ([Bigul](https://bigul.co/blog/algo-trading/navigating-slippage-in-algo-trading-your-guide-to-smoother-execution-in-india)), and NSE's own impact-cost regime accepts up to 0.50% on ₹10 crore baskets in Nifty-50 names ([NSE](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/indices-impact-cost)) — so the notes' honest band for a daily-traded delivery idea is **~0.35–0.65% per round trip just to break even**, with the caveat that no India-specific next-open execution study was found; the narrower 5–10bp/side band for our execution style is a reasoned estimate, not a documented number. The state has been tightening the screws throughout: Oct 2024's six F&O friction measures ([SEBI framework summary](https://v2.webnotes.in/sebi-fno-entry-barrier-rules-2024)), the Feb 2025 retail-algo framework ([SEBI circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html)), and an April 2026 STT hike (futures 0.02→0.05%; options 0.1→0.15%) that roughly doubled derivative friction again ([Zerodha bulletin](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/445377/revision-in-stt-securities-transaction-tax-from-1st-april-2026)). Survival rates are brutal in derivatives — **87.7% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY26** (₹91,685 crore aggregate; ~₹25,000 crore of it transaction costs), option sellers were the only strategy group with positive median returns on capital, and 99% of FPI/prop profits came from algo entities ([SEBI FY26 study PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2026/1787233506209.pdf), [ET](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/stocks/news/rs-91685-crore-gone-88-retail-investors-lost-money-in-fo-trading-in-fy26-even-after-strict-sebi-rules/articleshow/133381520.cms)) — with the loud caveat that SEBI has published **no comparable cash-delivery churn study**, so these loss rates are derivative-segment evidence, not delivery. The clearest self-published ledger in retail quant (Tradetron, Aug 2025–Aug 2026, 42,661 deployments, gross of charges) found only 29% of intraday versus 53% of positional deployments profitable, and its cost chapter showed the identical rule on 1-minute charts earning ₹8,094 where 15-minute charts earned ₹61,457 ([Tradetron](https://tradetron.tech/research/state-of-retail-algo-trading-india-2026.html)). The profile of documented retail winners: larger capital, positional/low-turnover, defined-risk selling, systematic cost-aware execution.

## The LLM-trading evidence audits to nothing tradable — and one live AI fund lost

The only LLM-in-equities result surviving peer review at a top journal (Lopez-Lira & Tang, *JFE*, Oct 2026) reports what its own authors scope as **non-tradable**: ~90% portfolio-day hit rates on the same-day initial reaction, plus a modest predictability of drift *concentrated in small caps and negative news*, and — decisive — "strategy returns decline as LLM adoption rises" ([arXiv 2304.07619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07619), [JFE](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2026.104335)). The famous agentic backtests are author-reported single-name or short-window artifacts: FinAgent's "92.27% on TSLA" ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383466825_A_Multimodal_Foundation_Agent_for_Financial_Trading_Tool-Augmented_Diversified_and_Generalist), venue unverified) and TradingAgents' ~26.62% cumulative on AAPL over ~5.5 months annualized to a "30.5%" claim ([tauric.ai](https://tauric.ai/research/tradingagents), [arXiv 2412.20138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20138)) carry their own repo's disclaimer that results are "not guaranteed to match any published figure" ([github](https://github.com/tauricresearch/tradingagents)). The 2026 audit of agentic trading found, across **19 closed-loop empirical studies: 1/19 with an explicit transaction-cost model, 1/19 handling survivorship, 15/19 with no reproducible artifacts, 0/19 fully reproducible** ([Xia et al., ESWA / arXiv 2605.19337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19337)). Objective mismatch is demonstrated, not hypothetical: an RL-fine-tuned agent improved classification accuracy on daily direction *and lost money*, from reward hacking on the proxy objective ("The Losing Winner," [NeurIPS 2025 workshop](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/132549)). And the one real-money "AI picks the stocks" product with a public NAV, AIEQ (IBM Watson, live since 2017), returned ~100% versus ~200% for the S&P 500 through Aug 2026 ([247wallst](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/26/can-ai-help-you-beat-the-stock-market-the-answer-may-surprise-you/), [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4915859-aieq-sophisticated-ai-driven-strategy-that-underperforms-reiterate-hold)) — the longest public test of an AI layer deciding the book, and it lost to the benchmark. Industry deployment confirms the division of labor: LLMs go to the analyst layer while decisions stay quant/human ([Hedgeweek Q1 2026](https://hedgeweek.com/news/hedge-funds-rank-ai-as-their-number-top-priority-but-experts-say-they-may-be-ignoring-this-blind-spot), [Bloomberg, Jul 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-01/ai-models-could-make-hedge-fund-trading-riskier-wall-street-researchers-warn)).

## The arithmetic we must beat: 55% breakeven, and an edge our sample rate can't even see

With near-symmetric payoffs on a ~4–5% volatile 5-session index-relative pick, cost c = 0.4% pushes the breakeven hit rate to **p\* = (L+c)/(W+L) ≈ 55%** (≈54% on a 5%-vol pick) — direct arithmetic from the notes ([SpreadSheetsHub table](https://spreadsheetshub.com/blogs/articles/trading-metrics-that-matter-expectancy)); payoffs are near-randomly symmetric (avg win ≈ avg loss ≈ 0.8σ at zero mean), so "asymmetry" does not rescue anything. Translated to skill: expected per-name edge e₀ ≈ IC × σ_pick ([AnalystPrep on Grinold–Kahn](https://analystprep.com/study-notes/cfa-level-2/state-and-interpret-the-fundamental-law-of-active-portfolio-management-including-its-component-terms-transfer-coefficient-information-coefficient-breadth-and-active-risk-aggressiveness)); the notes' IC ladder at σ=4% reads IC 0.03 → net −0.28%, **IC 0.05 → net −0.20%**, IC 0.10 → net 0.00, i.e. the hurdle demands **IC ≈ 0.07–0.10**, while real-world stock-selection signals typically deliver IC 0.02–0.05 and 0.10+ is exceptional. Our top-pick sample rate then multiplies the problem: detecting a mean edge of e at σ=4% needs n ≈ (2s/e)² settled trades — **324 trades (~13 months at 6 picks/week) to detect 0.5%**, ~1,600 (~5 years) to detect 0.2%, and ±3pp hit-rate precision needs ~1,056 picks (~3.5 years) ([Algorier](https://algorier.com/blog/minimum-number-of-trades-for-a-backtest), [PineConnector](https://www.pineconnector.com/blogs/pico-blog/how-many-trades-backtest-sample-size)). The minimum detectable edge in a year at our throughput is **~0.46% per trade (s=4%, 6/week) up to ~1.4% at slower/higher-σ cases** — so the notes' verdict is that a true +0.3% edge produces t = 1.3 after a year: we cannot see the winning outcome we are hoping for, in the year we will look. Meanwhile the DSR math says an unlogged multi-variant research process manufactures false positives — after 1,000 trials the *expected* best backtest Sharpe is ≈3.26 even if every strategy is worthless ([Bailey & López de Prado](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf)), and a per-trade SR of 0.125 from a genuinely 0.5% edge already sits below the expected maximum luck from just 10–100 unlogged variants. Published edges, pretenders and real alike, then decay: −26% out-of-sample and **−58% post-publication** ([McLean & Pontiff, *JOF* 2016](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jofi.12365)) — a haircut that alone kills private edges living near the cost hurdle.

| 2 | **Edge size vs the 0.4% wall** | IC 0.05 → ~0.2%/trade → net **−0.2%**; book-level −5 to −10%/yr at our turnover; decay haircuts kill edges near the hurdle | Long-only NIFTY-100, near next-open execution, 0.4% planning hurdle (0.238% pure costs + 5–20bp slippage band) | Structural unless IC is exceptional (0.07–0.10) or costs/turnover are engineered down |
| 3 | **Objective mismatch** | Higher direction-accuracy can coexist with lower net P&L ([Losing Winner](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/132549)) | p_outperform is accuracy-native; the bridge instead gates on **mean realized excess > 0.4%** — correctly the P&L objective | Partially structural; behavior already correct, mindset must follow |
| 4 | **Gated mechanics unavailable** | Latency/market-making/PFOF/premium capture need colo, licenses, capital; our class (daily directional, long-only delivery) is the retail-accessible one and the lowest-margin one | By design: long-only, no shorts, no options writing, NIFTY-100 large caps | Structural (accepted) |
| 5 | **Machine availability** | Every foregone observation at event time is unrecoverable in a forward-only design; survivorship/preregistration discipline is worthless with holes in it | **5 of 6 evening runs lost since Oct 30** (Nov 2 missed; Nov 4 none; Nov 5 daily 18:12 + catch-up 21:02 after 17 cards; Nov 6 daily 18:24, catch-up 21:41 with 0 cards) | **Ours to fix, immediately** |
| 6 | **HALT is correct** | Professionals gate hard exactly here: preregistration, deflated Sharpe, paper→live ladders ([TradersPost](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/backtesting-market-regimes-for-2026-strategies), [Bailey & LdP](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf)) | ≥30 settled cards, ≥10 bullish, hit rate >0.5, mean excess >0.4% before any intent; TRADE 0.6 unreachable until calibration unlocks (~50 settles) | Correct conduct; keep it |

One design asset deserves naming: the hash-chained, append-only, preregistered ledger **is** the trial registry the DSR math requires (the audit literature's 15/19-unreproducible papers didn't have one). Cost: pre-registration forever excludes Batches A/B and ~318 pre-November cards from the paper stream — that's the price of a clean stream, and professionals pay it deliberately. One measurement shortcut also deserves naming: the full card stream (~50–60 cards maturing/week, and eventually ~100 stocks × 2 horizons per day) measures **forecast IC** on thousands of independent-ish names within months — vastly faster than expectancy on ~300 top picks/year — with the caveat (per the notes) that overlapping holds and cross-correlation shrink effective sample, and IC is a leading indicator, not a substitute for the net-of-cost test.

## The honest path: settle the cards, then let the gates decide

The professional ladder is preregister hypothesis → walk-forward/OOS → deflated-Sharpe/multiple-testing acceptance → 3–6 months of paper with pre-declared criteria (40–60+ trades) → small live capital → scale ([QuanterLab](https://quanterlab.com/articles/getting-started-paper-trading), [Quantified Strategies](https://quantifiedstrategies.substack.com/p/incubation-of-trading-strategies), [QuantVPS](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/quantitative-trading-explained), [TradersPost](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/backtesting-market-regimes-for-2026-strategies)). Mapped onto our machinery, the sequence is: **(a)** fix host power management so the machine stays up — this is the only gap that wastes undetectable value every week it persists; **(b)** let the ledgers mature: 5-session cards from late-October batches have begun settling, 20-session cards settle into late November, and steady state brings ~50–60 maturing cards/week; **(c)** at ~50 settles per horizon, calibration unlocks and the probability band widens from [0.35, 0.65] — which is when the TRADE threshold becomes reachable, and the bridge — not us — decides whether any card deserves the label; **(d)** the pre-registered shadow stream accumulates top-3-per-horizon picks net of 0.4% to their own horizons; ~30+ settled picks is the earliest professional-scale reading, and its verdict is a bug-and-execution gate, not a statistical verdict; **(e)** only if mean realized excess clears the hurdle across ~1–2 full settled horizons does a tiny real-money test make sense, with pre-declared kill criteria; full statistical respect arrives only around ~1,000 settled picks and ~2–3 years. And the honest failure branch, stated without cushioning: if after 1–2 settled horizons the mean excess does not clear ~0.4%, the data will be saying this strategy class doesn't clear Indian delivery costs — and the research points to the fallbacks in order of evidence strength: shift the universe toward the one drift the best-audited LLM paper found tradable (small caps with negative news — [arXiv 2304.07619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07619)); stretch horizons and cut turnover, since India's documented winners are positional and Tradetron's nulls are all speed ([Tradetron](https://tradetron.tech/research/state-of-retail-algo-trading-india-2026.html)); cut execution cost per round trip, which converts directly into required IC (each −10bp of cost is +10bp of edge); or explicitly reposition Quant-Plus as a research/education instrument whose output is measurement, not P&L.

## Conclusion

Two things become clear from putting the four bodies of evidence next to our ledger. First, the complaint "why can't we achieve profitability" is aimed at the wrong week: with two settled outcomes, the system has no testable claim, and the only honest description of November 7 2026 is not "unprofitable" but "pre-evidence." Second, the parts of quant trading that print the most money are purchased with infrastructure and licenses, not cleverness — so the profit ceiling of a daily directional, long-only, delivery-pay class is set less by how good the forecaster is and more by arithmetic: cost per round trip, turnover, and an IC whose realistic band (0.02–0.05) sits under India's 0.4% wall. That reframes the goal: Quant-Plus's immediate deliverable is not profit but a defensible measurement — a preregistered, cost-net stream that can, within roughly a year of unbroken operation, *rule edges out* (and detect only large ones in). If that measurement eventually clears the hurdle, the path to scaled deployment remains the professional one; if it doesn't, the same measurement will have produced the evidence that redirects the system toward small-cap negative-news drift, longer-horizon low-turnover books, cheaper execution, or an education-and-research identity. Either way, keeping the machine running, unmodified gates and all, is not patience theater — it is the experiment.
